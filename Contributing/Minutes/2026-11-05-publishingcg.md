## Agenda

*Publishers have brought forward the requirement from the EU to report and document the use of AI in the production of published materials. There are two Github issue on this topic: [Reporting and documenting the use of AI in published documents](https://github.com/w3c-cg/publ-a11y/issues/814) and [Identifying AI contributions to EPUB content](https://github.com/w3c/epub-specs/issues/2833#issuecomment-6570781747)*

## Resume

* AI use covers a wide spectrum, from fully generated to AI-assisted content. The PCG's role is to collect use cases in publishing and track related work so the community is aware, our use cases are considered, and we can inform which vocabularies to use in which context.
* Candidate use cases: AI-generated alt text, synthetic voice in audiobooks, translation, and images with or without human review.
* Signalling could appear in the content or metadata. Reading systems and websites would handle display, and legal obligations vary by country.
* ONIX can already flag AI involvement, and a chunk of ONIX could be included in an EPUB.
